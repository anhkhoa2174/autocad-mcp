## Quy doi nhanh

- 1 hot desker can tham chieu **5 - 7 m2** (ban + ghe + loi di + share).
- 1 dedicated desker can **7 - 10 m2**.
- Phong meeting can **2.5 - 3 m2/nguoi**.
- Density coworking trung binh **6 - 8 m2/cho** (gop ca circulation va share).
